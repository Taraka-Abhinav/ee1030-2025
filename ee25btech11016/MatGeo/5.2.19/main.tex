\textbf{Question}:\ 
Solve the system of equations
\begin{align}
x + y = 14
\end{align}
\begin{align}
x - y = 4
\end{align}

\solution \

The equation of line:
\begin{align}
\vec{n^T}\vec{x}=c
\end{align}

Line L:
\begin{align}
\myvec{1 & 1}\myvec{x \\ y}=14
\end{align}

Line K:
\begin{align}
\myvec{1 & -1}\myvec{x \\ y}=4
\end{align}

These can be combined and written in matrix form:
\begin{align}
\myvec{1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1}\myvec{x \\ y} = \myvec{14 \\ 4}
\end{align}

The following augmented matrix can be solved by gaussian elimination
\begin{align}
\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 1 & 14 \\ 1 & -1 & 4}
\xleftrightarrow{R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - R_1}
\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 1 & 14 \\ 0 & -2 & -10}
\end{align}

Next, divide the second row by $-2$:
\begin{align}
R_2 \leftarrow \frac{R_2}{-2}: \quad
\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 1 & 14 \\ 0 & 1 & 5}
\end{align}

Subtract $R_2$ from $R_1$ to eliminate $y$:
\begin{align}
R_1 \leftarrow R_1 - R_2: \quad
\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 0 & 9 \\ 0 & 1 & 5}
\end{align}

So, the solution is
\begin{align}
\myvec{x \\ y} = \myvec{9 \\ 5}
\end{align}
