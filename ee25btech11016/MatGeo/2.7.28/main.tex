\textbf{Question:}\\
Find the area of the triangle whose vertices are $A(2,3)$, $B(3,5)$, and $C(4,4)$.

\solution\\
The area of a triangle with vertices $\vec{A}$, $\vec{B}$, and $\vec{C}$ can be calculated using the determinant of the vectors forming two of its sides. The formula is:
\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \det(\vec{A}-\vec{B}, \vec{A}-\vec{C}) \right| \]
The given vertices are represented by 2D vectors:
\[ \vec{A} = \myvec{2 \\ 3}, \quad \vec{B} = \myvec{3 \\ 5}, \quad \vec{C} = \myvec{4 \\ 4} \]
First, we find the two side vectors:
\begin{align}
    \vec{A}-\vec{B} &= \myvec{2 \\ 3} - \myvec{3 \\ 5} = \myvec{-1 \\ -2} \label{eq:vec_ab} \\
    \vec{A}-\vec{C} &= \myvec{2 \\ 3} - \myvec{4 \\ 4} = \myvec{-2 \\ -1} \label{eq:vec_ac}
\end{align}
Next, we calculate the determinant of the matrix formed by these two vectors as columns:
\begin{align}
    \det(\vec{A}-\vec{B}, \vec{A}-\vec{C}) &= \begin{vmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ -2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} \nonumber \\
    &= (-1)(-1) - (-2)(-2) \nonumber \\
    &= 1 - 4 = -3 \label{eq:determinant}
\end{align}
The area is half the absolute value of this determinant:
\begin{align}
    \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} |-3| = 1.5
\end{align}
Thus, the area of the triangle is 1.5 square units.
